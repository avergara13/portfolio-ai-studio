Loft OS

A governed operating system for moving AI-assisted work from human intent to verified closeout.

Scope, authority, runtime, integrations, evidence, and human control are treated as system requirements.

SCOPE

Explicit boundaries defining what the role is allowed to touch.

AUTHORITY

What the agent can decide vs. what strictly requires human release.

EVIDENCE

The verifiable artifacts that must exist before closeout is allowed.

Five interacting planes coordinating governed work.
