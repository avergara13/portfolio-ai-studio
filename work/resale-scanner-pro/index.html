THE OPERATING PROBLEM

Resale decisions happen under incomplete information. Market evidence is fragmented, acquisition cost is immediate, fees and shipping affect margin, and the operator still has to decide whether an item is worth buying.

01 CAPTURE

Item / cost / condition

02 MARKET EVIDENCE

Comparable listings + sold signals

03 FINANCIAL JUDGMENT

Expected value / fees / uncertainty

04 HUMAN DECISION

Buy / Pass

The Stopping Rule

The system is not designed to produce more AI output. It exists to help reach a bounded financial decision.

Market evidence + Acquisition cost + Fees / shipping allowance + Uncertainty limits ↓
